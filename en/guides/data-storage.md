# Where and how my data is stored

Your account data — properties, bookings, documents, settings — is stored on
servers in Germany.

## Backups

We keep regular backups, encrypted, on servers in the Netherlands. They are
deleted automatically at the end of the retention period (180 days).

## What is encrypted

The keys that connect your account to your booking channels and your invoicing
provider are stored encrypted, as are the backups.

## Who can see it

You, and anyone you invite to your account.

On our side, access happens only to support you or to solve a technical problem,
and it is always logged.

Nothing leaves on its own. Documents go to your invoicing provider and
declarations to AADE when you issue or submit them, by hand or through the
automatic invoicing you have turned on — that is what DeskBoy is for, and you
are in control of it. Automatic invoicing is a switch you turn on, and you can
see what's about to be issued before it goes out. The other providers (hosting,
email delivery, AI assistant) process data so the service can run; you will find
all of them, with their purpose and country, in the Privacy Policy.
