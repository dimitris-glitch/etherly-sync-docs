# Property Groups

Organise your properties into groups, e.g. by area ("Crete", "Attica") or by owner. Lists show them by group, and in the **Property** filter you pick a whole group with one click. Each property belongs to one group, and its invoicing settings stay as they are.

## Creating a group

1. In **Settings → Properties** press **New group**.
2. Give it a name.
3. Add properties from the list in the dialog.
4. Press **Save**.

The group's colour is set automatically; change it whenever you like from **Edit group**. You can have up to 20 groups.

## Assigning a property to a group

- From the property card, in the **Group** field.
- From the property's **⋮** menu → **Move to group**, with **Undo**.
- From **Edit group**, many at once — the quickest way to organise at the start.
- By dragging, from **Organise**.

If a property already belongs to another group, the app tells you before saving. Properties without a group appear under **Ungrouped**.

## Organising by dragging

Press **Organise**, next to **New group**: a handle appears on every property. Drag the property onto a group — or onto **Ungrouped** to take it out — and drop it. It moves straight away, with **Undo**. **Done** takes you back to the normal list. Dragging also works with the keyboard.

## Ordering properties inside a group

In **Edit group**, drag the properties by their handle into the order you want and press **Save**. The order applies everywhere the group is shown.

## Deleting a group

From the group's **⋮** menu → **Delete group**. Its properties move to **Ungrouped** and their settings stay the same.

## Groups on other screens

- **Property filter**, on **Bookings**, **Online Check-Ins** and **AADE declarations**: properties are shown by group, and a group's checkbox selects all its properties. You can combine properties from different groups; with nothing selected you see all. Each screen remembers its own choice.
- **Bookings → Departures today**: bookings are shown by group.
- **Settings → Online Check-In**, **Active Properties** and **cleaning-staff assignment**: properties are shown by group, and each group opens and closes with a click.
