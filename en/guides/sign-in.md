# How I sign in and how my account is protected

There are two ways into DeskBoy: your Google account, or a code we email you.

## With an email code

1. Enter your email and press "Send me a code".
2. Within seconds an email arrives with a six-digit code.
3. Type it on the screen and you are in.

The code is valid for 10 minutes and is used once. Without it, nobody can sign
in — not even us. The same email also carries a link: open it on the same device
and one tap does the work of the code.

If you cannot see it, check your spam folder too. After half a minute the screen
enables "Didn't get it?" so you can request a new one.

## Your devices

Every device you sign in from is added to the "Devices" list in your profile,
with the time it was last used. When a new one appears, we email you so you know.
From that same list you can sign out any device — a phone you lent out or lost,
for example. Signing it out takes effect within a few seconds.

## If you get an email about a sign-in you did not make

Sign in, revoke the device from the list, and write to us at
[security@deskboy.app](mailto:security@deskboy.app). The code that was sent to
you expires on its own within a few minutes — nobody signed in with it unless
they used it.
