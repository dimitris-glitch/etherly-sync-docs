# Language & format

**Settings → Language & format**

## App language

Ελληνικά or English. The same choice is available from your account menu, top right.

## Region

Sets how dates, times and amounts appear across the app — EU countries, the
United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia. With **Greece**:
`19/08/2026` · `14:05` · `1.234,56 €`.

The preview shows the result as you change the selection. Every change is saved
immediately.

## Customize format

For something other than your region's default:

- **Date** — `19/08/2026`, `08/19/2026` or `2026-08-19`
- **Time** — 24-hour (`14:05`) or 12-hour (`02:05 pm`)
- **Numbers** — `1.234,56`, `1,234.56` or `1 234,56`

Month and day names follow the app language.

## Where it applies

Across the app and in the emails you receive. Each recipient sees dates in their
own format — the daily report reaching your accountant arrives in theirs. Pages
opened by guests, cleaning staff and property owners follow each reader's own
language.

The calendar on a date field follows your browser settings.
